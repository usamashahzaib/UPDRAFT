# HOTDROP — player experience audit and closure plan

Date: 3 October 2026, Pakistan time. Working tree based on `4c70079a7e606a3cf5aa32962f6ab38a8d3790ec`, with extensive pre-existing staged and unstaged work.

**Verdict: continue controlled prototype testing; do not call this a complete commercial mobile shooter.** There is substantial implemented gameplay and test coverage. The presentation still exposes blockout art, the next-session motivation is thin, and the core online/mobile experience has not passed its recorded acceptance gates. More menu screens will not resolve those problems.

This is a source, saved-render, acceptance-evidence and automated-test audit. I did not personally play a phone session or a live multiplayer match. No Android device was attached. Visual judgments below are design judgments, not measured retention results. Suggested acceptance targets are explicitly distinguished from existing specification targets. No audit can guarantee commercial success or absence of every defect.

- Existing Unity renders: Home, challenge gameplay, challenge results, accessibility settings. These are editor captures, not current Android recordings. The capture helper renders cameras/canvas and omits the immediate-mode touch overlay: missing controls in these pictures are **not** evidence that phone controls are missing.
- The previous September 29 QA report, October 2 UI report, October 3 offline device checklist, Gate C and D records, and product performance requirements.
- Current backend suite rerun: **167 passed, zero failed, zero skipped**. Repository-root artifact: `.audit_tmp/player-audit-2026-10-03/PlayerAudit.trx`. NuGet vulnerability metadata was unavailable (NU1900); the pass is not a dependency-security check.
- Historical full Unity artifacts: `ProjectHotdrop/TestResults/RooftopFullEditMode.xml` records **296/296**; `RooftopFullPlayMode.xml` records **17/17**. These were inspected, not silently relabeled as fresh runs.
- Fresh full Unity PlayMode rerun: **17 passed, zero failed**, 198.57 seconds, process exit 0. Artifacts: `.audit_tmp/player-audit-2026-10-03/PlayMode.xml` and `PlayMode-retry.log`. This is editor gameplay coverage, not Android or live networking acceptance.

## Player-facing findings and exact fixes

Priority means order of work: P1 blocks a credible release experience; P2 materially reduces clarity, polish or replay motivation. Evidence labels matter: **Observed render**, **Source-proven**, **Acceptance gap**, and **Design proposal** do not mean the same thing.

| P01 / P1 | The scenic rooftop Home and primitive runner/arena do not present a coherent finished game. **Observed render:** `03_home_1920x1080.png`, `14_gameplay_1920x1080.png`. | Finish one coherent vertical slice: both runner variants on a shared animation standard; three distinct weapon silhouettes; readable pad armed/damaged/spent states; modular rooftop kit; matching lighting and UI treatment. Preserve collision and combat dimensions while replacing visual meshes. | Capture the same build from Home through a full match. Verify enemy, teammate, pad and zone recognition at actual phone size. Check final assets against the license register and Low-device budgets. |
| P02 / P1 | Online 3v3 is the dominant promise, but the recorded six-client gate remains unaccepted. **Acceptance gap:** `docs/acceptance/STEP_C.md`. For deliberately offline builds, make offline play the primary action and label online availability before the tap. Do not infer server health solely from configured URL presence. | Six clients finish repeated matches at 30/80/150 ms RTT, with loss/jitter; repeat disconnect during fire, pad launch, capture and respawn. Verify one avatar and one reward outcome per player, correct team membership and usable recovery/Home. |
| P03 / P1 | A technically working drill may still be hard or confusing on thumbs. **Acceptance gap:** all twelve device drills remain NOT RUN. Preserve completed records. | Each scenario returns to exactly one coherent screen/world, with released touch ownership, correct clock, honest saved status and no duplicate actors/results. | P09 / P2 | Large challenge instructions obscure a substantial upper-left portion of the arena. **Observed render:** the 1920×1080 Basic Route tracker is approximately 750 px wide and 214 px tall. | Show a short action plus progress during play; put the full description in Detail/Pause. | P10 / P2 | The replay progression is limited: authored defaults only award Bronze until medal thresholds are validated; contracts/mastery are disabled by default. **Source-proven intentional gating.** | Use local bests and chapter progress now. Offline achievements never masquerade as server inventory or currency. Do not turn on flags merely to fill a screen. |

| P13 / P2 | Accessible options exist, but presence of a toggle does not prove usable layouts or inclusive combat cues. **Source and acceptance gap:** settings offer left-handed controls, larger text/HUD and camera shake. | Test complete journeys with each option and combinations. Muted/audio-impaired play still communicates hit, reload, threat, objective and death state. |
| P14 / P1 | Account, party and reward recovery have backend tests but lack complete live player evidence. **Acceptance gap.** | Run guest/session recovery, sign-in, party leader/member transitions, queue cancellation, invite/join failure, result pending/retry and reconnect against staging. ## What is already stronger than the old audit suggests

Do not turn the previous 35 findings into a current defect count. Current source contains dedicated `Nav_*` navigation preservation, fractional `FireCadence`, aim-aware input activity handling, camera corrective collision sweeps and a pending-result outbox. These are implementation improvements, not proof that every original issue has passed real-device/online acceptance. Existing navigation, cadence, camera, result and touch tests are valuable regression protection.

Offline challenges now instantiate real player/weapon/pad/bot systems, rather than only menu entries. Tests verify objective conditions and local reward isolation. The explicit local-record labeling, disabled commerce and refusal to fabricate Silver/Gold tuning are sound product decisions.

## Ordered delivery plan

1. **Freeze and identify the candidate.** Save a baseline manifest and screenshots, preserve current work, and keep test artifacts separate. Fix invalid build/run documentation. No new feature scope.
2. **Close player-flow polish.** P06–P09: actionable Home goal, outcome-aware result actions, failure explanations and compact challenge tracker. Verify first-run, success, failure, retry, final challenge and Home transitions.
3. **Make one complete match feel finished.** P01/P03/P11: coherent arena/runner/weapon presentation plus human-completed controls, challenges and combat. Keep visual work inside measured mobile budgets.
4. **Prove reliability.** P02/P04/P05/P14: real six-client sessions, physical-phone thermal/input/interruption tests and staging account/reward recovery.
5. **Decide whether the game earns another session.** P10/P12/P15: measure the pad study and replay/retention before expanding progression or monetization.

A fix is closed only when its acceptance test passes on the identified candidate. “Code exists,” “screenshot looks better,” and “unit test passed” are useful evidence with different limits.

## Commercial release items outside this player audit

Publisher identity, signing, public support/privacy/deletion endpoints, asset provenance and purchase lifecycle remain separate release work already tracked by the project. This report does not provide legal, store-policy or dependency-security certification. Commerce remains gated; enabling it is not a remedy for weak replay value.

## Changes made in this audit

This report and fresh verification artifacts; the existing PlayMode capture tests also regenerate their UI evidence PNGs. No gameplay source, balance, feature gate, production service or account data was changed. The fixes above are a reviewable implementation and acceptance plan, not a claim that they have already shipped.
