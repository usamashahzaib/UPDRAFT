# Project HOTDROP Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Effective date:** [PLACEHOLDER: EFFECTIVE DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how **Techspirex** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and retains information when you play **Project HOTDROP** (the "Game") on Android or use related services.

This document is a draft and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before publication.

## 1. Information we collect

### Account and sign-in information

You may play as a guest or optionally sign in through Google Play Games. We collect or generate:

- a pseudonymous Project HOTDROP player identifier;
- guest-session and account-linking information needed to maintain your profile and progress; and
- if you choose Google Play Games sign-in, the account identifier and basic profile information Google makes available to the Game, such as your player name or avatar.

We do not receive your Google account password.

### Gameplay and analytics information

We collect pseudonymous gameplay analytics associated with your player identifier. This includes events and statistics needed to understand and operate the Game, such as:

- tutorial starts, progress, completion, and abandonment;
- matchmaking and queue activity, including queue duration, selected region, match start, match completion, disconnects, voluntary rematches, and whether bots filled a match;
- match statistics and outcomes, including match duration, mode, score, wins and losses, eliminations, deaths, weapon usage and performance, objective participation, and launch-pad placement or use;
- progression, cosmetic inventory, reward, and cosmetic-shop interactions;
- crash, error, performance, and network-quality information; and
- coarse device tier and coarse region.

We use a coarse region for service operation and analysis. We do **not** collect precise or device location.

### Purchase information

Optional cosmetic purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment method and card details. The Game does not receive or store complete card details. We may receive and retain purchase-related records such as the product purchased, purchase token or order reference, price and currency, purchase status, and time of purchase so that we can verify and deliver the item, prevent fraud, provide support, and meet accounting obligations.

### Information we do not collect

The Game does not collect:

- chat content (the Game does not provide text or voice chat);
- your contacts or address book; or
- precise or device location.

If you contact support outside the Game, we will receive the information you choose to include in that request, such as your email address and message.

## 2. Why we use information

We use the information described above to:

- create and maintain guest or linked accounts, save progress, and provide the Game;
- authenticate players and link an optional Google Play Games login;
- match players, operate multiplayer sessions, select an appropriate service region, and maintain network performance;
- deliver, verify, and restore cosmetic purchases;
- understand tutorial completion, retention, matchmaking times, rematches, balance, and other gameplay trends;
- diagnose crashes, errors, abuse, cheating, and security incidents;
- enforce our Terms of Service and protect players and the Game; and
- comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to perform our agreement with you, for our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Game, to comply with law, and with consent where consent is required.

## 3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed with service providers, authorities, and transaction participants, including:

- **Google Play Games and Google Play Billing:** for optional sign-in, purchase processing, purchase verification, refunds, fraud prevention, and related platform services. Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policies.
- **Photon:** for multiplayer networking and related online-service operation. Photon may process pseudonymous player identifiers, connection and session data, IP addresses, region, and technical diagnostics needed to provide networking services.
- **Service providers:** hosting, analytics, security, customer-support, and professional advisers that process information for us under appropriate obligations.
- **Authorities or other parties:** when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, enforce our agreements, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or complete a merger, financing, acquisition, or transfer of assets subject to appropriate safeguards.

Information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## 4. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

- raw, player-level analytics are retained for **30 days**;
- aggregated or de-identified analytics are retained for **180 days**;
- account and gameplay records are retained while your account is active and then deleted or de-identified following a valid deletion request, subject to the exceptions below; and
- purchase and transaction records are retained for the period required by applicable accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and legal requirements.

Backups and logs may take a limited additional period to cycle out. We may retain information when reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims; prevent fraud or abuse; comply with law; or complete a transaction you requested. We will restrict retained information to those purposes.

## 5. Account deletion and your rights

You may request deletion from the Game's account settings or through the Project HOTDROP account-deletion webpage: [PLACEHOLDER: PUBLIC ACCOUNT-DELETION PAGE URL]. You may also contact playhotdrop@proton.me. We may need to verify that you control the account before completing a request.

After a verified deletion request, we will delete the account and personal data associated with it, except information that we must or are permitted to retain for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or enforcement purposes. Aggregated or de-identified information that can no longer reasonably identify you may remain.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. To exercise a right, contact playhotdrop@proton.me. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

## 6. Children and age eligibility

Project HOTDROP is intended for players aged **16 or older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a person under 16 has provided personal information, contact playhotdrop@proton.me so that we can investigate and delete it as appropriate.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No system or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy, update the effective date, and provide any additional notice required by law. Material changes apply prospectively unless law permits otherwise.
