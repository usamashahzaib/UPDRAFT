# HOTDROP — production logo generation prompt

Use this prompt for the primary logo concept. Generate the symbol and wordmark on a clean presentation background first. App-store artwork, characters, and environmental scenes come later.

```text
Act as a world-class identity designer creating the definitive production logo for an original mobile game named HOTDROP.

BRAND AND PRODUCT

HOTDROP is a premium landscape mobile 3v3 objective sport shooter built around fast three-minute rooftop matches. Its defining mechanic is a compact player-deployed launch pad that creates a temporary airborne shortcut. Either team may use an armed pad, so every shortcut creates opportunity and risk. Players move through a bright near-future industrial rooftop arena named Switchyard, contesting rotating Relay zones above a coastal megacity.

The emotional promise is:
“Build a shortcut. Take the objective. Watch who follows.”

The brand should communicate upward momentum, intelligent movement, precision, teamwork, speed, controlled risk, and earned mastery. It should feel premium, adult, athletic, optimistic, physical, modern, and original. It is an organized rooftop competition, not a military operation and not a fantasy battle.

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Create an iconic, ownable logo that can become recognizable before the viewer reads the name. It must work as:
- a Google Play app icon at very small size,
- a startup-screen mark,
- a horizontal game wordmark,
- a one-color stamp on equipment and clothing,
- a launch-pad emblem inside the game,
- a social avatar and favicon,
- and a clean vector mark for animation.

The logo must attract through clarity, tension, balance, and a memorable silhouette—not through excessive detail, glow, weapons, characters, explosions, or visual noise.

CORE SYMBOL CONCEPT — THE HOTDROP ROUTE

Construct one compact geometric symbol from only three integrated ideas:

1. A low circular or broken-ring base representing the deployable launch pad.
2. A precise upward-curving route rising from that base, representing controlled lift, traversal, and tactical choice.
3. Intentional negative space that subtly forms the letter U without drawing a literal standalone U.

The upward route should begin inside or immediately above the pad ring, accelerate upward, and resolve in a clean directional cut. Its path may lean slightly forward to suggest useful movement rather than vertical hovering. Add at most one small route interruption, wind tick, or landing-oriented cut if it improves distinctiveness. The ring and route must feel like one engineered symbol, not separate icons placed together.

The silhouette should imply “launch, curve, commit” in one glance. It may also hint at two route choices or two teams sharing one path, but this must remain secondary and must not make the mark symmetrical, crowded, or confusing.

Do not use a literal airplane, bird, feather, rocket, jet, parachute, cloud, tornado, mountain, superhero emblem, shield, helmet, gun, bullet, crosshair, target reticle, skull, crown, flame, lightning bolt, generic chevron, or standalone upward arrow. Do not create a generic esports crest.

FORM AND CONSTRUCTION

- Use a bold, flat, geometric vector style.
- Build the icon from two to four major shapes.
- Prefer strong filled shapes and carefully controlled negative space over thin outlines.
- Use consistent optical weight, clean joins, deliberate cuts, and compact proportions.
- Avoid tiny details, distressed edges, textures, bevels, 3D extrusion, glass effects, chrome, shadows, lens flares, gradients, and unnecessary symmetry.
- The mark must remain recognizable at 24 px and when viewed as a black silhouette.
- Keep enough internal spacing so the symbol does not close up when reduced.
- Do not enclose the complete symbol inside a generic badge, shield, hexagon, or app-icon squircle.
- The symbol should feel engineered like premium athletic equipment and transit wayfinding, not decorative science fiction.

WORDMARK

Set the exact name `HOTDROP` in uppercase beside the symbol as a horizontal lockup.

Create a custom geometric sans-serif wordmark with excellent readability. Use wide, confident proportions, controlled spacing, open counters, and subtle forward movement. The letterforms should feel aerodynamic and athletic without becoming an illegible techno font.

Customize only one or two meaningful details:
- The `U` may echo the launch-pad bowl or negative-space geometry.
- The `A` may contain a restrained route cut or open counter.
- A subtle shared angle may connect selected terminals across the word.

Do not replace multiple letters with symbols. Do not stretch, heavily italicize, stencil, outline, fragment, or over-customize the word. Avoid military stencil lettering, cyberpunk typography, racing clichés, razor-sharp aggression, and fonts resembling established shooter franchises.

The symbol must also work alone without the wordmark. The wordmark must remain readable when the horizontal logo is reduced to approximately 160 px wide.

COLOR SYSTEM

Primary production palette:
- Hotdrop Cyan: #30D5E8
- Deep Flight Navy: #101827
- Light neutral: #F4F7FB

Use cyan as the active signal and navy as the structural foundation. Use the light neutral only for reversed applications. Do not introduce additional colors into the core logo. Objective Gold #FFD166 belongs to gameplay objectives and rewards; exclude it from the primary logo so the brand mark does not compete with the objective system.

The logo must work perfectly in these applications:
1. Cyan symbol and wordmark on deep navy.
2. Deep navy symbol and wordmark on white or light neutral.
3. Pure black one-color version.
4. Pure white reversed version.

PRIMARY PRESENTATION TO GENERATE NOW

Create one professional brand-identity presentation on a clean deep navy background:
- Center the primary horizontal lockup: symbol on the left, `HOTDROP` wordmark on the right.
- Beneath it, show the square icon symbol alone at a smaller scale.
- Include one tiny monochrome reduction to prove small-size recognition.
- Maintain generous empty space.
- Use perfectly flat artwork with crisp vector-like edges.
- No game characters, weapons, city scene, UI panels, phone mockups, merchandise mockups, cinematic background, slogans, decorative labels, grid measurements, or explanatory paragraphs.
- The only visible brand text must be the exact word `HOTDROP` once in the primary lockup.

QUALITY STANDARD

The result should feel like a global entertainment identity capable of carrying a long-term game franchise, while remaining achievable as clean SVG geometry. It should be distinctive enough that the icon can be recognized without text, calm enough to sit inside a premium interface, and energetic enough to promise movement.

Before producing the final image, silently test the design against these questions:
- Can the symbol be described from memory after a three-second glance?
- Does it express upward route movement without becoming a generic arrow?
- Does it visually connect to the launch-pad mechanic?
- Does the hidden U appear as a rewarding discovery rather than a gimmick?
- Is the silhouette readable at 24 px?
- Does it work in one color?
- Is the word HOTDROP spelled exactly and immediately readable?
- Does it avoid similarity to existing shooter, airline, sportswear, aerospace, and esports identities?

Return a polished, minimal, flat, vector-style identity presentation. Prioritize originality, memorability, silhouette, and small-size performance over decoration.
```

## Required export request after choosing the concept

```text
Rebuild the selected HOTDROP logo as clean editable vector geometry. Deliver:
1. Primary horizontal lockup.
2. Symbol-only square icon.
3. Wordmark-only version.
4. Stacked lockup.
5. Cyan-on-navy, navy-on-light, black, and white variants.
6. Transparent-background SVG and PNG exports.
7. App icon master at 1024 × 1024 with at least 18% optical safe margin; do not bake a rounded-square mask into the artwork.
8. Horizontal logo master at 2400 × 800.
9. Minimum-size proof at 24 px icon and 160 px horizontal lockup.
10. Clear-space guide based on the height of the wordmark U.

Use paths rather than external fonts or linked images. Remove hidden layers, raster effects, clipping mistakes, stray points, and unnecessary anchor points. Preserve exact colors #30D5E8, #101827, and #F4F7FB. Spell HOTDROP exactly. Do not add a trademark symbol.
```

## Selection rule

Reject the result even if it looks attractive when any of these are true:

- The icon could belong to an airline, VPN, generic esports team, fitness app, or cryptocurrency.
- The launch-pad origin is not visible.
- The symbol depends on glow, gradients, texture, or 3D depth.
- The wordmark becomes unreadable below a large display size.
- The mark resembles an existing game or sportswear identity.
- The design contains more than four meaningful symbol elements.
